# Structural solver validation status

Updated 20 September 2026 following the independent counterexamples in
[the critical evaluation](https://github.com/jalonso1979/puremacro/blob/v4.3.0/reviews/2026-09-19-critical-evaluation/REVIEW.md).
See also the [live Dynare and native OECD follow-up](https://github.com/jalonso1979/puremacro/blob/v4.3.0/reviews/2026-09-20-independent-validation/REPORT.md).
The subsequent [trade-accounting correction](https://github.com/jalonso1979/puremacro/blob/v4.3.0/reviews/2026-09-20-trade-accounting/REPORT.md)
adds an independently checked, opt-in producer/purchaser model.
Feature availability, numerical convergence, and independent replication are
separate claims. Passing a residual check does not validate an economic model.

| Surface | Supported calculation and acceptance contract | Evidence and remaining limits |
|---|---|---|
| Trade equilibrium | `accounting="consistent"` uses homogeneous factor costs, producer/purchaser values, output-revenue taxes, actual final-expenditure tax shares, fixed baseline foreign saving and an explicit numeraire. Convergence also checks the omitted goods equation and realized foreign balances. | Independent scalar two-country solution and a conserving OECD aggregation, with budget/GDP identities and currency scaling. Supports NumPy, Leontief/CES and lump-sum rebates; other fiscal recycling, capacity costs and GPU are explicitly unavailable in this mode. The default `accounting="legacy"` preserves historical MATLAB conventions. See [equations and scope](trade_accounting.md). |
| Tariff receipts | In consistent mode, all duties use exporter prices and the complete bilateral schedule, are paid once by buyers and rebated once to importing households. Metadata exposes domestic-tax receipts and producer/purchaser ledgers. | `tariff_revenue_mode="schedule"` alone retains legacy valuation. Historical `legacy_national` fiscal receipts can differ from reported transaction tariffs. Use `accounting="consistent"` for new counterfactuals. OECD production-column TLS is modeled as an output tax; the data do not identify a full product/VAT schedule. |
| Keller PAC | Complete starting/target intermediate and final-demand schedules; dense full-system continuation; residual-based final acceptance. `condensed=True` is explicitly unavailable. | Newton/PAC agreement for a small tariff experiment. The notebook's scalar fold illustration is not evidence that the public CGE solver traverses an economic fold. |
| Caliendo–Parro | The identity shortcut requires tariffs, iceberg costs and deficit closure to be individually unchanged. | Offsetting tariff/iceberg changes must retain revenue. No new live replication of the authors' published quantitative results was performed. |
| Tariff games | Explicit `metric="hicksian_ev"` uses consistent accounting, one fixed zero-tariff baseline, consumption-normalized gains/regret and fixed actions in payoff matrices. Full-interval grid/refinement and final simultaneous deviations determine numerical convergence. Audited Newton → hybrid → continuation recovery preserves the model and tolerance; failed deviations raise without a payoff. | Independent scalar CES equilibrium and primal expenditure reference on a 41-by-41 tariff grid, plus false-convergence/recovery tests. This benchmark reaches its tariff ceiling; neither interior optima nor global Nash uniqueness are established. Historical objective defaults remain unchanged. See [usage, evidence and limits](trade_policy.md). |
| Hicksian consumption welfare | `compute_hicksian_welfare` evaluates expenditure-function EV/CV from consistent-accounting equilibria, with explicit consumption categories and purchaser prices. A separate endpoint Shapley attribution uses prices, factor income and fiscal transfers. | Independent origin-level expenditure minimization, all six attribution orderings, duality checks, currency scaling and the OECD aggregation. Investment is excluded; native OECD C includes government consumption. This is conditional consumption welfare, not lifetime utility or a causal efficiency decomposition. See [derivation and evidence](trade_welfare.md). |
| Historical EV decomposition and theorem certification | `decompose_hicksian_ev_3way` and `verify_theorems_1_to_4` still raise `NotImplementedError`. | Their former TOT/Alloc/TariffRec formulas and theorem conclusions were unsupported. The new welfare interface does not relabel its derived channels as those quantities. Historical tests remain non-executed expected failures, not economic validation. Legacy tariff-game utility aliases and flexible-model proxies are not covered by the new evidence. |
| MRIO adapters | Missing source files fail by default. Generated data require explicit opt-in. Calibrations retain source, year, units, generation seed or file hash, conversion assumptions, and adjustment summaries. | OECD 2023 regular / 2019 native CSV: all 77 countries × 45 sectors ingested by labels and calibrated, with original OUT margins, signed inventory changes and repairs recorded. Six final uses map to C/I/Cx to match the investment closure. A conserving 3-region × 3-sector aggregation passes baseline, accounting and tariff counterfactual checks. Other native editions/providers and full-size GE remain unverified. FIGARO has only a hand-authored harmonized fixture. Labor/capital shares of 2/3 and 1/3 remain assumptions. |
| MRIO productivity | Strongly connected blocks, positive-diagonal shifted iteration, and Collatz–Wielandt bounds; a positive resolvent vector strengthens bounds for small badly scaled matrices. An interval crossing the viability threshold is unresolved, not a successful certificate. | Periodic and reducible matrices are covered. The scalar spectral estimate alone is not a productivity certificate. |
| DSGE order three | Risk slopes include state timing, Hessian chain terms and third-derivative variance contractions. Parsed models use symbolic derivatives; callable-model fallback has finite-difference accuracy. | Five models executed in live Dynare 7.0 at orders two and three, including correlated shocks, nonlinear states and an RBC model. All unfolded tensors, risk corrections, covariance and common-innovation simulations/moments agree (maximum tensor error 1.15e-13; path error 2.70e-13). Mixed-derivative assembly now preserves each index permutation. This is a bounded benchmark, not general parity. MATLAB completed exports but crashed on shutdown; see reproduction notes. |
| Dynare parity dashboard | Orders one and two compare steady states and all requested tensors (`ghx`, `ghu`, `ghxx`, `ghxu`, `ghuu`, `ghs2`), aligned by labels. Missing/nonfinite/malformed input is unavailable. Supplied moments must actually compare. | DR-only comparisons do not establish moment or innovation-covariance parity. Metadata distinguishes unavailable covariance and unrequested moments. Self-generated serialization fixtures are internal tests, not external Dynare oracles. The parity CLI cannot consume references and exits with an explanatory error; use the Python API. |
| Markov-switching DSGE | Coupled-equation residuals are recomputed at the returned matrices. Failed solutions have unavailable shock impacts, stability radii and ergodic moments. | A real-root failure must not return finite equilibrium covariance or a stability certificate. Finite moments also require stability after convergence. |
| VFI projections | Euler collocation, FEM, Smolyak and spline flags require finite final residuals within the requested tolerance. Optimizer termination alone is insufficient. | A fitting-node residual does not establish off-grid accuracy, feasibility, complementarity or global optimality. Adverse domains can return useful approximations with `converged=False`. Bellman variants have their own iteration criteria. |
| Collocation auxiliary values | Policy evaluation uses the model's productivity and CRRA utility parameters. | Tested against the deterministic log-growth steady-state value. Custom return functions retain their own economic meaning; boundary clamping limits interpretation outside the chosen domain. |
| Deep Macro | Detached-target Euler time iteration. The current-state forward cache is restored before the weight update. | A complete training-step gradient is checked by finite differences. Training success is calibration/seed dependent; even the default deterministic ten-country trajectory can fail the held-out Euler criterion. A small training loss alone is not convergence. |
| VFI sensitivities | IFT linear solves using central-difference residual Jacobians: semi-analytic derivatives. | Accuracy depends on differencing steps and conditioning. Heterogeneous-agent distribution/GE sensitivities are unavailable until differentiated KFE and coupled equilibrium equations are implemented. |

The 107-case validation gallery contains 59 internal, 29 analytical, 13 package,
five SciPy and one published-reference case. It has multiple tolerance classes
and contains no trade cases; separate trade tests must be assessed separately.
The gallery does not certify every feature, every backend or every deployment.

Puremacro ships no compiled extension of its own. NumPy/SciPy and other base
packages include compiled components; the mandatory dependencies also include
pandas, Matplotlib and requests. Pyodide importability, realistic browser
execution, GPU agreement and bitwise reproducibility require different tests.
