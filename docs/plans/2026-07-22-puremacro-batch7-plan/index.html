SIC divisions: farm; ag services/forestry/fishing; mining; construction; manufacturing; transport & public utilities; wholesale; retail; FIRE; services; government. - Exposure vector: z-scored shares for ~8 aggregated divisions (drop farm/ag-services tiny ones into 'other'). Outputs irf_shiftshare.csv, fig_phase6_shiftshare.*, phase6_manifest/summary, DRAFT.md §7 + limitations update (the shift-share item resolves).
