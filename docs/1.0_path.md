# Roadmap and API compatibility

PureMacro has passed 1.0. This page replaces the old pre-1.0 checklist;
its URL is retained for existing links. As of 2026-11-15, **6.5.0 is the
published release** on PyPI (succeeding 6.4.0, 6.3.0, 6.2.0, 6.1.0, and 6.0.1),
while this source tree and changelog target **6.6.0**.
Source tags 5.8.0, 5.9.0 and 6.0.0 were not published to PyPI. Consult the
[changelog](https://github.com/jalonso1979/puremacro/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)
and the version returned by `puremacro.__version__` when reproducing a result.

## Development priorities

Milestones shipped across **6.2.0–6.6.0** and remaining validation boundaries:

1. **Release engineering, sovereign grid scope & own-data policy briefs (6.1.0–6.5.0):**
   The `6.1.0` baseline consolidated the GMM engine, asset-pricing core, explicit
   `debt_grid=...` sovereign default API ([sovereign default](sovereign_default.md),
   [extension validation](sovereign_extensions_validation.md),
   [sovereign brief](brief_sovereign_risk.md)), consistent-accounting trade GE
   ([tariff validation](tariff_validation.md)), and wheel/typing release checks.
   Subsequent `6.2.0`–`6.5.0` releases added cumulative local-projection fiscal
   and tariff policy briefs with own-data inputs, optional ENIGH quintile mapping,
   and elasticity-grid workflows ([fiscal brief](first_brief_fiscal.md),
   [tariff brief](first_brief_tariffs.md)). Published sovereign extension
   moments and a full competitive collateral model remain outside the validated
   scope.
2. **Equilibrium derivatives, continuation, simulation-aware SMM & identification-robust inference (6.2.0–6.5.0):**
   `puremacro.core.EquilibriumSystem`, `linearize_equilibrium`, and
   `continue_equilibrium` provide dense implicit-function sensitivities and
   pseudo-arclength fold continuation ([equilibrium linearization](equilibrium_linearization.md),
   [branch continuation](equilibrium_continuation.md); statistical confidence sets
   for equilibrium branches remain separate). `puremacro.structural` adds
   `fit_simulated_moments` accounting for both empirical sampling error and Monte
   Carlo variance ([simulation-aware SMM](simulation_aware_smm.md)) alongside
   identification-robust point-null S-tests and moment confidence sets
   ([identification-robust inference](identification_robust_inference.md)), plus
   fail-closed Markov/nonfinite guards in `puremacro.sovereign.smm`.
3. **One-asset HANK benchmark, bounded second-order HANK & financed fiscal closures (6.1.0–6.5.0):**
   The published one-asset HANK **general equilibrium** passes independent
   nonlinear and grid/horizon checks ([benchmark](hank_equilibrium.md)).
   Experimental `solve_hank_second_order` adds quadratic state terms and
   anticipated-variance corrections with grid residual/mass/budget audits for
   small full-basis models ([second-order HANK](hank_second_order.md); reduced
   bases, two assets, productive capital, global accuracy, and welfare remain
   outside scope). Releases **6.5.0–6.6.0** add explicitly financed fiscal HANK
   closures in `puremacro.models.hank_fiscal` (`solve_hank_fiscal`,
   `compare_fiscal_closures` under `balanced_budget` and Leeper `debt_rule`
   tax financing; see [structural models](models.md) and
   [validation status](STRUCTURAL_VALIDATION_STATUS.md)) with joint budget/asset
   residual and asset-truncation diagnostics, alongside the empirical LP–HANK
   household incidence bridge in `puremacro.models.hank_distributional_bridge`
   (`bridge_hank_distributional`, [distributional bridge](hank_distributional_bridge.md)).
4. **Panel CCE Mean Group, auditable trade reallocation incidence, macro freshness monitor & workbench/CLI/MCP server (6.3.0–6.6.0):**
   Releases **6.5.0–6.6.0** add Common Correlated Effects Mean Group Local Projections
   (`puremacro.lp.cce`, `estimator="mean_group"` / `cce_mg_panel_lp`; see
   [local projections](lp.md)) with Pesaran (2006) cross-unit inference,
   non-converged batch guards in `run_scenario_batch`, and dynamic two-country,
   two-sector household reallocation incidence with OECD ICIO calibration in
   `puremacro.trade.reallocation_incidence`
   (`evaluate_transition_household_incidence`, [trade reallocation incidence](trade_reallocation_incidence.md)).
   Bounded provenance and freshness reporting
   (`puremacro.fetch.freshness.connector_freshness_report`) audits the flagship
   connector cohort across zero-network `cache`/`recorded` modes and
   rate-budgeted `live` probes ([connector health](CONNECTOR_HEALTH.md)), while
   `puremacro.macro_monitor` (`run_macro_monitor`, [macro monitor](macro_monitor.md))
   bundles offline-first freshness, `preflight_panel`, rolling AR(1) nowcasts,
   and bounded LP transmission checks into RFC 8259 JSON and SHA-256 manifests.
   Finally, `puremacro.workbench` (`run_empirical_structural_workbench`), CLI
   tooling (`puremacro workbench`, `hank-fiscal`, `robust-set`), and the
   Pyodide-safe stdio MCP server (`puremacro.mcp_server`, `puremacro-mcp`)
   compose preflight, sensitivity grids, identification-robust moment sets, IRF
   comparisons, scorecards, standalone HTML / bilingual bundle exports, and
   explicit solver diagnostic propagation
   ([workbench and scorecards](workbench_and_scorecards.md),
   [MCP server](mcp_server.md)).

Completed development milestones do not establish every model or calibration,
or promise shipment in a particular release. The
[research reliability guide](research_reliability.md) records the current
boundary between replication evidence, internal checks and demonstrations.

## Compatibility policy

Public exports and result-class field names are recorded in
`tests/fixtures/public_api_snapshot.json`. Gate 3 requires an intentional,
reviewed snapshot update for additions and other surface changes. The snapshot
checks names, not numerical correctness, function signatures or semantic
compatibility; those require tests and review.

For the stable API, additions belong in a minor release. Renames, removals,
changed argument meanings or incompatible return shapes require a major
release. Before removal, retain a callable deprecated form for at least one
minor release, with a `DeprecationWarning` naming the fully qualified symbol,
replacement and removal version. Document numerical bug fixes in the changelog,
including changed reference results; do not preserve an incorrect answer merely
to keep a fixture unchanged. Compatible internal refactors and fixes can ship
in patch releases.

The established stable scope includes VAR (except regime-switching and GVAR),
local projections, inference, identification, the Klein solver, volatility,
cycles, cointegration, factor models, KORV GMM, MIDAS, spectral methods,
synthetic control, wavelets, realized volatility and labor-share estimators.
The research surfaces previously excluded from the stability promise remain
experimental: other structural/research models (including HANK and sovereign
extensions), regime VAR/GVAR, causal ML, dynamic programming/VFI, spatial GE,
OccBin, narrative/uncertainty, SMM, Bartik and research data-pipeline builders.
Their changes must still be explicit, tested and documented. Experimental
status is not a claim of independent economic validation.

Private modules and non-exported helpers are implementation details. Teaching
wrappers have separate optional dependencies. Examples are shipped in the wheel
and checked as workflows, but do not acquire a stable estimator API merely by
being examples. Fetchers depend on external provider schemas and are best-effort;
offline fixtures and schema checks document their expected inputs.

## Release evidence

Follow [RELEASING.md](https://github.com/jalonso1979/puremacro/blob/main/RELEASING.md).
Release checks cover the supported Python/OS matrix, public API, synchronized
versions, Python 3.11 syntax and native/Windows typing, the Pyodide import
contract, optional numerical reference suites, and representative installed-wheel
examples. Full notebook execution and live Pyodide smoke tests remain separate
checks with their own recorded results. A successful wheel smoke run is not a
substitute for either.
