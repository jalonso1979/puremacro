    Structural solver validation status

    Updated 23 September 2026 with rows for the MRIO engines ported from the IO research workspace (native tables, the condensed Leontief model, the nested-CES block Newton, household preferences, parameter continuation, reduced stability, the dynamic MRIO and the stacked Newton-Krylov), for the bundled-table provenance, consistent accounting on real OECD tables, the flexible trade route and dynamic determinacy, and with the rows revised by the post-4.3.0 audit fixes. The 20 September 2026 update followed the independent counterexamples in the critical evaluation. See also the live Dynare and native OECD follow-up. The subsequent trade-accounting correction adds an independently checked, opt-in producer/purchaser model. Feature availability, numerical convergence, and independent replication are separate claims. Passing a residual check does not validate an economic model.

    Surface Supported calculation and acceptance contract Evidence and remaining limits
    Trade equilibrium accounting="consistent" uses homogeneous factor costs, producer/purchaser values, output-revenue taxes, actual final-expenditure tax shares, fixed baseline foreign saving (in numeraire units by default, or as a share of world factor income with foreign_saving_units="world_income") and an explicit numeraire. Newton, sparse LU, Newton-Krylov, Broyden and Keller stop only when the solved residuals and the audited physical equations (the omitted goods equation and the realized foreign balances) meet tol; their finite-difference steps scale with each unknown (h_j = eps * max(|x_j|, s_j)), so resolution does not depend on the currency unit. SciPy hybrid/LM use their own stopping rule and are audited afterwards. Independent scalar two-country solution and a conserving OECD aggregation, with budget/GDP identities and currency scaling. test_consistent_oecd_empirical_aggregation passes on Python 3.13.12 / NumPy 2.4.6 / SciPy 1.18.1 (tariff Newton: 6 iterations, max residual 4.8e-8 at tol = 1e-5 M USD; before the fix it stopped at 8.3e-6 and was rejected at 1.03e-5). The OECD fixture in USD (validate=False, tol 10 USD) and a seeded 20-country x 11-sector nonnegative synthetic table in USD converge (tests/test_trade_solver_audit_fixes.py). With nonzero foreign saving the default closure depends on the country order (country A's EV on the two-country welfare table is -27.55% of consumption with A first and -45.99% with B first); "world_income" removes the dependence (to 7e-11). Full-size native GE (77x11 or larger) remains unverified; the bundled 77x11 legacy table is rejected by consistent accounting. Supports NumPy, Leontief/CES and lump-sum rebates; other fiscal recycling, capacity costs and GPU are explicitly unavailable in this mode. The default accounting="legacy" preserves historical MATLAB conventions. See equations and scope.
    Tariff receipts In consistent mode, all duties use exporter prices and the complete bilateral schedule, are paid once by buyers and rebated once to importing households. Metadata exposes domestic-tax receipts and producer/purchaser ledgers. tariff_revenue_mode="schedule" alone retains legacy valuation. Historical legacy_national fiscal receipts can differ from reported transaction tariffs. Use accounting="consistent" for new counterfactuals. OECD production-column TLS is modeled as an output tax; the data do not identify a full product/VAT schedule.
    Keller PAC Complete starting/target intermediate and final-demand schedules; dense full-system continuation; residual-based final acceptance. A scalar tau_target (any 0-d real input) also applies to final-demand imports unless scalar_applies_to_final_demand=False, and the choice is recorded in metadata["scalar_tariff_convention"]. condensed=True is explicitly unavailable. Newton/PAC agreement for a small tariff experiment. The notebook's scalar fold illustration is not evidence that the public CGE solver traverses an economic fold, and the solver docstrings no longer claim a fold at sigma_fold ~ 0.1238: on notebook 64's two-country calibration at sigma = 0.1238 and a 25% tariff, PAC records no fold event and plain Newton also converges (8 iterations). No traversal of an economic fold has been demonstrated. metadata["fold_points"] holds merged events: consecutive tau_lambda <= 0 detections form one event with first_step, last_step, n_detections and a kind (lambda_reversal, factor_price_boundary or no_lambda_reversal), and metadata["fold_detections"] keeps the raw count. At sigma = 0 and a 232% tariff on the same calibration a factor price goes to zero: PAC stalls near lambda = 0.992 without converging and records one factor_price_boundary event of 55 detections, which 4.3.0 listed as 55 separate fold points. The Leontief Hawkins-Simon gate also applies for sigma > 0, where CES unit costs are at most the Leontief cost, so it can reject solvable CES schedules.
    Caliendo–Parro The identity shortcut requires tariffs, iceberg costs and deficit closure to be individually unchanged. Offsetting tariff/iceberg changes must retain revenue. No new live replication of the authors' published quantitative results was performed.
    Tariff games Explicit metric="hicksian_ev" uses consistent accounting, one fixed zero-tariff baseline, consumption-normalized gains/regret and fixed actions in payoff matrices. Full-interval grid/refinement and final simultaneous deviations determine numerical convergence. Audited Newton → hybrid → continuation recovery preserves the model and tolerance; failed deviations raise without a payoff. A Leontief schedule whose Collatz–Wielandt lower bound is at least one is refused before any attempt (PolicyEquilibriumError.viability); other screening outcomes are recorded in metadata["policy_viability"]. Independent scalar CES equilibrium and primal expenditure reference on a 41-by-41 tariff grid, plus false-convergence/recovery tests. This benchmark reaches its tariff ceiling; neither interior optima nor global Nash uniqueness are established. Historical objective defaults remain unchanged. See usage, evidence and limits.
    Hicksian consumption welfare compute_hicksian_welfare evaluates expenditure-function EV/CV from consistent-accounting equilibria, with explicit consumption categories and purchaser prices. A separate endpoint Shapley attribution uses prices, factor income and fiscal transfers. Independent origin-level expenditure minimization, all six attribution orderings, duality checks, currency scaling and the OECD aggregation. Investment is excluded; native OECD C includes government consumption. This is conditional consumption welfare, not lifetime utility or a causal efficiency decomposition. See derivation and evidence.
    Historical EV decomposition and theorem certification decompose_hicksian_ev_3way and verify_theorems_1_to_4 still raise NotImplementedError. Their former TOT/Alloc/TariffRec formulas and theorem conclusions were unsupported. The new welfare interface does not relabel its derived channels as those quantities. Historical tests remain non-executed expected failures, not economic validation. Legacy tariff-game utility aliases and flexible-model proxies are not covered by the new evidence.
    MRIO adapters Missing source files fail by default. Generated data require explicit opt-in. Calibrations retain source, year, units, generation seed or file hash, conversion assumptions, and adjustment summaries. The FIGARO, EXIOBASE, WIOD and Eora harmonized loaders deliver C/I/Cx with the foreign-balance closure on gross fixed capital formation (I); their Cx is signed inventories and valuables (EXIOBASE: plus its export column), not residents' purchases abroad, as recorded in metadata["final_use_mapping"] and metadata["final_use_semantics"]. load_raw_45sector_icio checks the MD5 of the file it resolves and refuses the corrupted data_2020_SML.csv export. OECD 2023 regular / 2019 native CSV: all 77 countries × 45 sectors ingested by labels and calibrated, with original OUT margins, signed inventory changes and repairs recorded. Six final uses map to C/I/Cx to match the investment closure. A conserving 3-region × 3-sector aggregation passes baseline, accounting and tariff counterfactual checks. The FIGARO/EXIOBASE/WIOD/Eora harmonized loaders are verified on synthetic fixtures only; parity with native archives is unverified (the native FIGARO and EXIOBASE files are read by puremacro.trade.mrio). Other native editions/providers and full-size GE remain unverified. Labor/capital shares of 2/3 and 1/3 remain assumptions.
    MRIO productivity Strongly connected blocks, power iteration shifted by a small positive diagonal (shift=1e-3; 4.3.0 used the maximum row sum and left real tables unresolved), Collatz–Wielandt bounds, and inverse-iteration refinement with a positive resolvent vector for unresolved blocks of up to 1024 rows. An interval crossing the viability threshold is unresolved, not a certificate. check_hawkins_simon_viability reads tariff schedules exactly as solve_trade_equilibrium does and returns a four-field ViabilityResult; solve_policy_equilibrium refuses a Leontief schedule whose lower bound is at least one before any solver attempt. Periodic, reducible, near-critical (rho = 0.998) matrices and the bundled 77x11 table at tariff rates 3.65-10 match dense eigenvalues (tests/test_trade_ingestion_audit_fixes.py); the real OECD 2019 A certifies rho = 0.634194 and 1.55 A certifies 0.983001. The scalar spectral estimate alone is not a productivity certificate. No wall-time bound is guaranteed (0.02-0.11 s per schedule on the bundled table).

    Native MRIO tables (puremacro.trade.mrio) Label-driven readers for OECD ICIO 2023 (read_oecd_native, corrupted data_2020_SML.csv digest refused, unknown editions warned), Eurostat FIGARO 2026 (read_figaro_native, bounded rounding repairs recorded) and EXIOBASE 3.8.2 (read_exiobase_native, CSR; archive members, or an extracted directory passed with archive=, checked against the archive size and CRC-32 entries; a directory read alone is warned as unauthenticated); registered digests are bound to their year. regularize_table accepts a table only when the identity guard (median row error <= 1e-5, empty zero-output cells), the residual-tax gates (<= 1e-4 of world TLS, <= 1e-2 of cell output) and the fob world-balance gate (<= 1e-9 of world GDP) pass, and returns every gate value with the offending cells; under strict=False a failed table is marked regularized=False and aggregation and the calibration bridge warn. aggregate_mrio is exact P M P^T with conservation and a 1e-8 identity gate; coarse_tariff_rates implements the IO 11o/11b rules for nonnegative rates; to_calibration_matrix builds labour and capital rows that calibrate_trade_model accepts (both factors strictly positive at every active cell of a regularized table; negative factor value added raises). Hand-derived fixtures in the official FIGARO and EXIOBASE layouts; parity with the vendored IO table_from_arrays, aggregate_icio, coarse_11o/coarse_11b in a subprocess (max difference 0, 2.8e-17 for 11o rates) and agreement with dynamic_model.native_data and calibrate_databases_2019.figaro on the real 2019 files to floating-point summation order (0 on every OECD and EXIOBASE array and against calibrate_databases_2019.figaro, at most 9.3e-10 on FIGARO output against native_data), slow-marked: OECD 25 phantoms, world GDP 87,854,087; FIGARO 67 phantoms, 9 floors, and the regularized 50 x 64 table passes calibrate_trade_model; EXIOBASE 1,084 phantoms, 89 floors, extracted core authenticated against the registered archive. Limits quoted from the IO records: "active_mask means positive reported output; it is not an economic feasibility certificate"; FIGARO "Territorial consumption is retained; a residence bridge has not been validated"; EXIOBASE "This 2019 release is a nowcast; no stronger measurement claim is implied"; residence_welfare_comparable: False for all three. No equilibrium is solved on FIGARO or EXIOBASE (EXIOBASE is not calibrated: the bridge densifies a 7,987-square Z); other editions and years, and GTAP (GPL-3 HARPY, licensed data) are outside the validation. The bundled 77x11 table derives from the corrupted OECD file (world VA 7.05e11 versus 7.97e7 in the clean 2020 release; 35.5% of intermediate cells equal, value added equal in 1 of 847 cells); its parity suites are internally consistent regressions, not empirical checks. See native tables.
    Consistent accounting on real OECD tables accounting="consistent", compute_hicksian_welfare, solve_policy_equilibrium and the exact CES block Newton require nonnegative final-use coefficients. The bundled 77x11 table has three negative merged investment cells (min afd -0.144, min theta -0.133) and the regularized granular 45-sector calibration also carries signed inventory aggregates, so consistent accounting runs today on hand-built tables and on the frozen OECD 3-region/3-sector fixture only. A signed-inventory final-use category is the follow-up that lifts this; puremacro.trade.condensed carries signed inventories exogenously and is the supported route for full-size tables meanwhile.
    Condensed one-factor Leontief tariff model puremacro.trade.condensed.solve_condensed eliminates prices and outputs exactly (two LU factorizations), solves the 2N system (log w, Y/Y0) to max|r| <= 1e-12 with the dropped Walras residual <= 1e-10, gates existence with a Collatz-Wielandt bound rho(B_tau) < 1 - 1e-3 (inconclusive bounds raise, never claim nonexistence; multipliers below one are refused so the bound at the full duty covers every continuation stage), and with certify=True (default) accepts a state only after an independent ten-block raw-flow certificate (zero profit, goods clearing, factor markets, budgets, income, revenue identity, closure, current account, world adding-up, numeraire) at 1e-10, phantom cells entering with their true output ratio. certify=False results are diagnostic and never passed. Measures are numeraire-free by construction; separate_baseline_tariffs stacks increments on observed duties. Benchmark reproduction at 1e-14, certificates at 1.1e-13 (synthetic 3x4), 9.8e-13 (OECD 3x3 fixture) and 2.7e-13 (bundled 77x11 bridge), certificates of natural-unit tables with one to six phantom cells, certificate fault injection at relative 1e-5, EV-split additivity 1e-12, numeraire invariance 1.8e-11, inventory-duty neutrality under the factor closure, unit homogeneity, exact effective rate (tariffed and untariffed inventories), continuation/arclength/multistart/fallback behaviours and subprocess parity with the vendored IO engine: bitwise on the synthetic table (also with baseline duties, the world-GDP closure with a country-wage numeraire, fixed investment and untariffed inventories), within 1e-12 on the OECD 3x3 fixture and the 77x11 bridge (observed 6.4e-14 on wages and prices, 4.3e-13 pp on headline measures). On the clean OECD 2019 release at native 77 x 45 resolution a uniform 10% U.S. merchandise duty reproduces the published IO rebuild figures exactly (consumer prices relative to wages +0.722154%, EV -0.005924% of base GDP; Agregar 11 sectors +0.705964% and -0.009092%), certificate 6.9e-13. Quoting the IO documentation: "No global uniqueness, universal fold threshold, or global Newton guarantee is asserted."; "Numerical residual checks do not establish global equilibrium existence, uniqueness, or optimality."; "These are floating-point verification criteria, not formal interval root enclosures." Arclength and multistart are evidence, not proofs. No economic turning point has been exhibited: the fold monitor is exercised by a synthetic determinant sign change and by an existence boundary (a 500% U.S. duty on the synthetic 3x4 economy passes the existence gate, but the equilibrium with a positive U.S. wage ends near a 259% duty, where continuation stalls and FoldDetected fires on a numerically singular Jacobian); this is not a universal fold threshold. One composite factor, Leontief technology and static EV that includes investment in absorption: results are not comparable one-to-one with accounting="consistent". The coarse 45-to-11 tariff rules and the OECD reader are in puremacro.trade.mrio, not in this module, and the paper's scenario registry is not ported; 45-sector runs need dense 3465 x 3465 factorizations and are not a browser target. See usage, equations and limits.
    Nested CES block Newton solve_ces_block_newton and continue_tariff_homotopy solve the consistent-accounting equations with a benchmark-normalized three-tier nested CES technology (plus the K/L nest) using the exact Jacobian: two M x M LU factorizations and a (3N+1) macro Schur complement, damped Newton with an admissibility filter. One acceptance test, shared with the postprocessing audit: the scaled imposed residual is at most tol, every level row (including the omitted goods equation and the realized foreign balances) is at most tol * max(scale), and an independent flow certificate is at most 1e-8. Every homotopy stage passes the same tests, and failures raise CESNewtonError with the history, the stages and the last accepted result. Complex-step exact-inverse oracle at non-equilibrium points (6e-14 on the hand-built and OECD 3x3 tables, 2e-12 at 20x11), central differences of _accounting.evaluate at flat technology (1e-6), row-by-row residual parity (1e-12), Leontief and flat-CES limits against the finite-difference solvers, benchmark reproduction, Euler/Shephard identities, ledgers within 1e-10 times the largest benchmark scale, a 66-solve acceptance sweep, certificate sensitivity (each key trips on the violation it names; an Euler-violating technology is refused), homogeneity, currency scaling, an independent dense root and a self-generated OECD golden. A synthetic 77x11 table solves in under a second for a mild 10% case (a few seconds for origin elasticity 4). The IO limits apply verbatim: "The proof is a conditional local identity, not a convergence or global-equilibrium theorem"; "The discrete checks do not certify a continuous branch between stages. ... failed procedures are not nonexistence certificates"; "all CES elasticities are explicitly stylized". The IO audit accepted 12 of 21 native elasticity profiles and left 9 unresolved (residuals 1.7e-4 to 1.4e-3). A direct Newton at origin elasticity 4 on the random 20x11 table fails and needs the homotopy. Under other random schedules the iterate is pressed against zero investment and the error names the binding category and country; in the case examined the nearby root has negative investment (fixed foreign saving exceeds the investment share of income). Unit origin elasticity on the OECD 3x3 fixture is a near-singular point of the benchmark Jacobian (smallest singular value 4.8e-6 at 0.99, checked by central differences of _accounting.evaluate) where the block Newton fails. Those cases raise and are documented, not solved. Prohibitive multipliers (1e8) put the level rows on a rounding floor above the default bound; a looser tol is needed. Hicksian welfare is available only at flat technology; parity with the IO one-factor DEK model is not claimed. See derivation and evidence.
    Household preferences and welfare puremacro.trade.household evaluates fixed-basket, Cobb-Douglas, Stone-Geary and CES sector-composite demand from an exact benchmark-normalized expenditure function; compute_household_welfare returns EV = e(p0,u1) - e(p0,u0) and CV = e(p1,u1) - e(p1,u0), positive for gains, with percentages on the base household budget. calibrate_household imposes Engel adding-up by an audited repair and requires 0 < lambda <= 1/max(eta); fit_supernumerary_share is a bounded regional WLS projection with reported residuals. Bridges re-audit consistent results exactly as compute_hicksian_welfare (an optional tau_fd is resolved and cross-checked against the recorded schedule). Legacy results must meet their recorded solve tolerance, are valued at the state prices in x_sol, and the solve's tau_fd is audited against the stored p_fd, which identifies national rates only: origin- or sector-specific legacy schedules raise unless trust_tau_fd_detail=True, in which case only their category aggregates are verified. Domain violations raise instead of clipping. Twelve IO behavioral/duality tests ported; analytic Slutsky matrices against central differences (5e-8), primal SLSQP expenditure minimization (1e-6), general-base and reversed welfare identities, currency scaling, flexible-module parity at benchmark income (1e-12, unit targets and a 2-D mu_s = 1 - lambda*eta with nonunit targets and a regional lambda), its off-benchmark divergence and its Slutsky asymmetry at income ratios 0.8, 1 and 1.2 (0.15, 0.06, 0.02 against about 2e-8 exact), Hicksian-welfare reproduction with fixed baskets (1e-10, categories 0 and 2, square and non-square 3x2 tables), an independent final-demand flow certificate (1e-12), legacy Leontief identity, legacy default-tolerance solves, residual gate and tampered-field rejections, and bit-identical in-process parity with the IO module. Quoting the IO documentation: the calibration "is a disclosed transfer and reconciliation, not native microdata estimation"; "a calibrated parameter is not thereby empirically identified for 2019"; LES "substitution restrictions are therefore substantive: matching income elasticities and projecting compensated own-price targets does not reproduce a general CDE substitution matrix"; "These remain static policy comparisons, not lifetime welfare". The supernumerary share is not identified by IO data (None defaults to 0.5 and the assumption is recorded in metadata); legacy sector-specific schedules are taken on trust when explicitly allowed (a different schedule with equal aggregates shifts the Cobb-Douglas EV by 12 percent on the synthetic table); bridged EV/CV inherit the equilibrium's numeraire and foreign-saving conventions (country-order dependent with nonzero foreign saving); bundled OECD category 0 includes government consumption; no GTAP CDE targets are bundled; the IO GE embedding, tariff continuation and local-stability diagnostic are not ported; flexible-model results are not bridged. See household preferences.
    Parameter continuation and multistart continue_parameter/sigma_path follow one model parameter (the intermediate elasticity) at fixed complete tariff schedules with adaptive natural-parameter steps (halve on failure, grow after stages of at most fast_iterations iterations, stop at min_step keeping the last audited state); try_starts tries named starts at one exact target. A stage is accepted only if the state declares convergence, carries consistent accounting, lump-sum transfers and exactly the requested sigma and schedules, passes the consistent-accounting re-evaluation of all equations at its recorded tolerance with every reported field equal to the recomputed equilibrium to 1e-8, has an audited residual at most the requested tol, and, unless allow_fallback=True, was solved by Newton from the warm start rather than through the fallback ladder or from another seed. sigma_path solves a missing start with the full audited ladder (the start is audited, not followed). Unresolved paths raise ParameterContinuationFailure with the last audited state and the policy solver's attempts; unresolved multistarts return no state.     Markov-switching DSGE Coupled-equation residuals are recomputed at the returned matrices. Convergence is diff <= tol (absolute), or every residual entry within tol or within rtol (64 eps) of the magnitudes of its own cancelling terms (componentwise backward error; the smallest certifying rtol is reported as relative_residual). Failed solutions have unavailable shock impacts, stability radii and ergodic moments. A real-root failure must not return finite equilibrium covariance or a stability certificate. Finite moments also require stability after convergence. Tested cases: uniform rescaling by up to 1e9; one equation rescaled by 1e6 and 1e9 on three models (the O(1) equations still meet tol and T stays within 1e-9 of the unscaled solution); variable units spanning 1e14; O(1) models (including a user-tightened tol) bit-identical to the absolute gate.
    Stacked-time Newton-Krylov solve_stacked_newton_krylov accepts a path only when the stacked residual satisfies |F|_inf <= tol * s (s the steady-block scale) and the next Newton step is below 1e-10 * max(1, |Y|_inf); step_tol=None drops the step test (with jacobian_scale=1.0 this is the IO native_solver._newton rule). The Newton move is bounded in path units (0.75 * max(1, |Y|_inf)) and accepted by a 24-trial Armijo line search. A direction returned at the Krylov cap is recorded (status and recomputed true linear residual) and line-searched like any other, as in the IO engine; it is not an error by itself. Failed line searches, iteration limits, errors or nonfinite values inside a linear solve, a residual at a noise floor that meets the tolerance while the step test cannot, and a steady-state stacked matrix that cannot be factored raise StackedNewtonError with the last state attached and the cause in converged_by. Block-Thomas pivots with a LAPACK condition estimate below n * eps, or a factorization whose one-vector backward error exceeds 1e-10, fall back to SuperLU (recorded in the preconditioner name). When both inputs are StackedNewtonResult objects, compare_horizons requires converged inputs, identical boundaries and the same exogenous path held beyond the short horizon; arrays, DataFrames and PerfectForesightResult inputs are compared as given.     VFI projections Euler collocation, FEM, Smolyak and spline flags require finite final residuals within the requested tolerance. Optimizer termination alone is insufficient. A fitting-node residual does not establish off-grid accuracy, feasibility, complementarity or global optimality. Adverse domains can return useful approximations with converged=False. Bellman variants have their own iteration criteria.
    Collocation auxiliary values Policy and value use one CRRA curvature (sigma, alias gamma) and the model's productivity in the Euler, Coleman, Bellman and policy-evaluation blocks. User return_fn/euler_residual_fn parameters are passed through uninterpreted. Tested cases: the deterministic log-growth closed form under every spelling; the Bellman identity under CRRA(2) and CRRA(3); Euler/Bellman policy agreement (1.1e-6) at sigma = 2; the IFT gamma sensitivity against the sigma column and an FD re-solve. Boundary clamping limits interpretation outside the chosen domain. Since 4.5.0 puremacro.vfi.splines and puremacro.vfi.smolyak follow the same sigma-then-gamma rule; solve_smolyak(method="bellman") covers only log utility with full depreciation and raises otherwise.
    Deep Macro Detached-target Euler time iteration. The current-state forward cache is restored before the weight update. A complete training-step gradient is checked by finite differences. Training success is calibration/seed dependent; even the default deterministic ten-country trajectory can fail the held-out Euler criterion. A small training loss alone is not convergence.
    VFI sensitivities IFT linear solves using central-difference residual Jacobians: semi-analytic derivatives. Accuracy depends on differencing steps and conditioning. Heterogeneous-agent distribution/GE sensitivities are unavailable until differentiated KFE and coupled equilibrium equations are implemented.

    The 110-case validation gallery (release 4.5.0, all passing) contains 59 internal, 31 analytical, 13 package, five SciPy and two published-reference cases. It has multiple tolerance classes and contains no trade cases; separate trade tests must be assessed separately. The gallery does not certify every feature, every backend or every deployment.

    Puremacro ships no compiled extension of its own. NumPy/SciPy and other base packages include compiled components; the mandatory dependencies also include pandas, Matplotlib and requests. Pyodide importability, realistic browser execution, GPU agreement and bitwise reproducibility require different tests.
